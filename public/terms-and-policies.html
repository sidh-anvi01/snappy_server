Your information

Privacy Notice

This notice describes the information the current SnapPy app is built to process. The person or organization operating your server is responsible for its deployment, access, and retention practices.

Information the app handles

Account and profile

Name, email address, username, gender supplied at registration, profile photo, and About text.

Conversations and media

Messages, shared image or audio media, sender and recipient, timestamps, and read status.

Status updates

Status images, their owner, and creation and expiry times. Status images are intended to leave the active feed after 24 hours.

Calls and device connections

Call setup and connection information needed to establish app-supported calls. This notice does not promise that calls are recorded or that they are end-to-end encrypted.

How information is used

  • to create accounts, authenticate users, and maintain profiles;
  • to deliver messages, media, status updates, and call setup;
  • to show account and status information to signed-in users of the instance;
  • to protect, troubleshoot, and maintain the service;
  • to meet legal obligations and respond to valid legal requests.

Who may receive information

Information is available to the people you communicate or share with, as needed for the feature. The server operator and its hosting or infrastructure providers may process information to run the service. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or needed to address abuse, security incidents, or the protection of users.

Storage, security, and encryption

Account and communication data are processed by the server that hosts your SnapPy instance. Passwords are designed to be stored as hashes, not as readable passwords. The operator should use secure transport (HTTPS/TLS) and appropriate access controls in production; protection depends on the actual deployment.

SnapPy does not currently promise end-to-end encryption. Do not treat messages, media, or calls as private from the server operator, and do not share sensitive information unless you trust the operator and have verified the deployment's security.

Retention and your choices

You can edit your name, profile photo, and About text in the app. Status updates are configured to expire from the active feed after 24 hours; message and account retention depends on the instance operator. Ask that operator about access, correction, export, or deletion of your information.

The current prototype may not provide a working server-side self-service account deletion flow. If the in-app delete option does not complete successfully, contact the person or organization that operates your SnapPy instance before relying on deletion.

Children's privacy

SnapPy is not designed for children who are too young to use an online communication service under the laws that apply to them. The instance operator should provide appropriate age and consent processes before offering the service to children.

Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated when the app or its data practices change. A materially different deployment may need an additional notice from its operator. Check the date at the top of this page for the current version.

Questions or privacy requests?
